# Mike 0.5.8 — rename and archive conversations in the terminal

- `mike-cli` gets Mike's page controls for conversations (my-euglena-apps
  ticket 193): `/rename TITLE` names the open conversation (`/rename N
  TITLE` one from `/conversations`), `/archive [N]` puts one away — kept,
  out of the list — and `/unarchive [N]` brings it back;
  `/conversations archived` lists the archived ones. Mike trims the title,
  cuts it to 80 characters and refuses one that looks like a password; the
  refusal is shown as it came.
- One turn at a time per conversation: a message sent while Mike is still
  working on that conversation (from this or another device) is not sent;
  the CLI says so and follows the running turn instead.
- The service adds `/v1/chat/rename` and `/v1/chat/archive`, passes
  `archived` to the conversation list, answers a busy conversation with
  409 `busy` and the running `turn_id`, and advertises `rename_archive` in
  its status, so an older service is told apart ("upgrade mike-service").
