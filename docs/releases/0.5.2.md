# Mike 0.5.2 — Mike CLI stands on its own

- `mike-cli --sign-in` signs in with your codelovesme account in the
  browser, starting the shared service when it is not running; `--account`,
  `--switch-account`, `--sign-out`. Plain `mike-cli` offers to start the
  service and to sign in when either is missing.
- The prompt is a line editor: ↑/↓ history kept between runs, Mike's
  progress printed above what you are typing, and messages sent while Mike
  works wait their turn (`/unqueue` takes them back).
- The ide's built-in AI chat is gone (ide 0.23.0); run `mike-cli` in its
  terminal tab instead, as you would `claude` or `codex`.

Service and Desktop are unchanged from 0.5.1.
